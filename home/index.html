Between Closets

TRADE FASHION, CREATE STYLE, REDUCE WASTE

Between Closets is a shared fashion platform. Instead of buying new, users circulate pieces through a heart-based system that values both condition and styling potential. You can exchange items, restyle them, and ask for simple advice. It connects different closets in a practical and easy way. What was once yours can become someone else's favorite again.
